# Mockup round notes (superseded)

The design system that came out of these mockups now lives at the root of the repository:

- [`DESIGN.md`](../../../DESIGN.md): how Jauge looks.
- [`docs/design/behaviour.md`](../../../docs/design/behaviour.md): how it behaves.

This folder holds the working mockups (throwaway code). Three directions were tried: A "Le cadran" (a dial of stations under a needle), B "La carte" (the map with a row of cards) and C "Le reçu" (a price sign and pump receipt). B was chosen and refined into B3, the version the documents above describe.
