# A place chosen by hand is never treated as the person's position

When someone looks at another town, the first mockup placed them on a station in the middle of that town and calculated detours from there, as if their device had reported that spot. The numbers looked precise and meant nothing. We now keep two cases apart. With a real position from the device, distances start from the person. With a place chosen by hand, the app shows an overview first (the map of the place, every price, a list sorted by posted price, no ranking by savings), and the station the person then taps becomes the starting point: it is the reference, and distances and savings are measured from it.

The cost is one more tap before savings appear when looking at another place. In exchange the app never shows a saving it cannot justify.

## Considered options

- Use the centre of the town as a stand-in position: rejected, it invents a position.
- Rank by posted price only when there is no position: kept as the overview, but not as the end state, because the detour is the point of the app.
